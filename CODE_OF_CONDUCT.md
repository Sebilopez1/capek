# Code of Conduct

Capek follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be respectful and welcoming, assume good faith, critique ideas and code rather than people, and don't
harass anyone. This applies in issues, pull requests, Discussions and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior makes you uncomfortable, email the maintainer at **sebastianlopez0599@gmail.com**. Reports are
read only by the maintainer and kept confidential. You can also use GitHub's "Report content" option on any comment.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block people who break this code, following the
Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban).
